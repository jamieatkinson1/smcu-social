# SMCU Communications Desk — Operations

## Publish a communication

1. Sign in through Cloudflare Access and open **Communications**.
2. Open the communication, check its content and artwork, and resolve every publishing-readiness reason.
3. Assign or confirm its campaign if needed.
4. Open **Publishing**. In **Ready**, select **Publish**.
5. Choose Shopify, Instagram, and/or Facebook. For social, choose a future schedule when required.
6. Review the exact destination list and confirm once.
7. Read each destination result. A success stays complete even if another destination fails.

## Understand and recover from a failure

- **Not connected:** open **Settings**, restore that connection, then retry only the failed destination.
- **Channel disconnected:** reconnect the Instagram or Facebook channel in Buffer, then retry.
- **Content or artwork blocked:** return to the communication, correct the listed item, and reopen Publishing.
- **Communication changed:** refresh Publishing and confirm the current version.
- **Service busy / timeout:** wait briefly and retry the failed destination. Do not create a second communication.
- **Scheduled:** Buffer owns the social queue. Check or cancel the post in Buffer when plans change.

## Daily close

Check **Publishing** for unresolved failures, confirm the recent publication, then use **Log out** in the sidebar (or the mobile header area). Cloudflare Access ends the session and returns to sign-in.
